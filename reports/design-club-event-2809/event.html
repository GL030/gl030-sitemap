WKND X MOVE ROOF

Friday, October 9th, 2026
Start: 10:00 PM
Weekend Club | Located on the 15th Floor · Rooftop
(please check our Instagram for updates)

On October 9th, MOVE ROOF joins WKND for a night dedicated to House Music, movement, and the special energy of Berlin nightlife high above the city.

Hosted by Misha Svirid and Phonique, this edition brings together a carefully curated sound, a stylish crowd, and the kind of atmosphere that makes a Friday night at Weekend Club feel unique. MOVE ROOF is about rhythm, connection, and letting the music guide the night from the first track to the final moment.

Expect a refined journey through Variety of House Music — warm grooves, uplifting rhythms, melodic moments, and driving late-night energy designed to keep the dancefloor moving with style and intention.

A night for familiar faces, new encounters, and everyone looking for quality House Music in one of Berlin’s most unique nightlife settings.

Please note that our venue is not fully barrier-free. Elevator access is available up to the 15th floor, but the rooftop on the 17th floor can only be reached via a staircase. An accessible restroom is located on the ground floor.

Dein Eventguide für Berlin

Seit 2010 liefert Gästeliste030 den täglichen Überblick über das Berliner Nachtleben: Party, Hip Hop, Electro, Day Party, Open Air, Halloween, Silvester – mit Terminen, Startzeiten und vergünstigten Gästelistenplätzen. Mehr als 1,8 Millionen Gästelisten-Einträge sind seitdem über uns zusammengekommen. Alle Termine stammen direkt von Clubs und Veranstaltern und werden laufend aktualisiert.

Was ist das echte Berlin? Für uns das tägliche Nebeneinander von Gegensätzen: Technoclub neben Rooftop-Bar, Späti neben Sternelokal. Genau dafür gibt es diese Seite – jeden Tag, für jede Nacht.
